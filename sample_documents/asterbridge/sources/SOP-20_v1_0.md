# Operations Management Control and Readiness Procedure

Document SOP-20 | Version 1.0 | Approved | Effective 2026-09-01
Owner: Managing Director | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: Operations Manager
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: No scheduled expiry

## Purpose and application
Give the Operations Manager a repeatable process for service readiness, approval decisions, incident oversight and corrective action.

Role mandate: Own service delivery, control exceptions and staff readiness. Reports to: Managing Director. Department: Management. Delegated authority is limited to the steps below; independent financial or policy approval remains with the named approver.

## 4.1 Review daily readiness
REQ-SOP-20-01 | Mandatory
The Operations Manager must review staffing, dispatch capacity, system status and unresolved critical incidents before each operating day. Any restriction must be communicated to affected teams.
Evidence: Daily readiness record and communicated restrictions.

## 4.2 Approve controlled exceptions
REQ-SOP-20-02 | Mandatory
The Operations Manager must record reason, scope, compensating control, owner and expiry before approving a permitted process exception. An exception cannot waive a prohibition or requirement reserved to another authority.
Evidence: Time-limited exception decision.

## 4.3 Ensure independent approval
REQ-SOP-20-03 | Mandatory
The Operations Manager must independently review payment and settlement evidence before approval. If personally involved as preparer, approval must be referred to the Managing Director.
Evidence: Approval record showing preparer and independent approver.

## 4.4 Lead critical incidents
REQ-SOP-20-04 | Mandatory
The Operations Manager must acknowledge critical escalations, appoint an incident lead and coordinate recovery under POL-09 and POL-10. Delegation does not remove oversight responsibility.
Evidence: Incident acknowledgement and lead assignment.

## 4.5 Review employee readiness
REQ-SOP-20-05 | Mandatory
The Operations Manager must review mandatory learning, practical evidence and unresolved gaps before authorizing independent restricted work. A completed checklist with missing evidence must be rejected.
Evidence: Readiness decision and evidence references.

## 4.6 Control policy changes
REQ-SOP-20-06 | Mandatory
The Operations Manager must identify affected requirements, learners and operational records before approving a policy revision. Affected training must be reviewed and stale content withheld from new assignment.
Evidence: Change impact assessment and training update decision.

## 4.7 Close corrective actions
REQ-SOP-20-07 | Mandatory
The Operations Manager must assign an owner and due date to corrective actions and verify effectiveness before closure. Overdue actions must be escalated to the Managing Director.
Evidence: Action register and effectiveness evidence.

## 4.8 Review improvement portfolio
REQ-SOP-20-08 | Recommended
The Operations Manager may rank employee suggestions monthly by expected benefit, effort and measurable outcome before selecting a pilot.
Evidence: Optional improvement review.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
