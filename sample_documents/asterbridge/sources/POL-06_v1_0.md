# Customer Data Privacy and Handling Policy

Document POL-06 | Version 1.0 | Superseded | Effective 2026-07-01
Owner: Operations Manager | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: All employees
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: 2026-09-01 (exclusive)

## Purpose and application
Limit personal-data use to approved operational purposes and ensure exports, disclosures and deletion requests remain controlled.

## 4.1 Temporary export removal
REQ-POL-06-01 | Mandatory
Employees must delete approved temporary customer-data exports within 48 elapsed hours after the approved task ends. A documented preservation hold suspends deletion only for the records identified in that hold.
Evidence: Export register with task-end and deletion timestamps or hold reference.

## 4.2 Minimum necessary data
REQ-POL-06-02 | Mandatory
Employees must access and collect only the customer fields needed for an assigned task. Full identity documents must not be requested for routine parcel tracking.
Evidence: Task purpose and field-access justification.

## 4.3 Recipient verification
REQ-POL-06-03 | Mandatory
Employees must verify the requester using the approved workflow before disclosing shipment details. A shipment number alone does not authorize disclosure of the recipient address or contact details.
Evidence: Verification outcome recorded without copying secret answers.

## 4.4 Approved transfer
REQ-POL-06-04 | Mandatory
Employees must transfer personal data only through approved company systems to authorized recipients. Personal messaging accounts and publicly accessible links are prohibited.
Evidence: Authorized recipient and approved transfer record.

## 4.5 Training data
REQ-POL-06-05 | Mandatory
Employees must use fictional or irreversibly anonymized customer examples in training and demonstrations. Replacing a name alone does not anonymize a record containing a real telephone number or address.
Evidence: Reviewed training sample with identifying fields removed.

## 4.6 Privacy requests
REQ-POL-06-06 | Mandatory
Employees must log access, correction or deletion requests and refer them to the Operations Manager on the same working day. Employees must not promise deletion before checking preservation requirements.
Evidence: Request record, referral and eventual decision.

## 4.7 Accidental disclosure
REQ-POL-06-07 | Mandatory
Employees must stop an unintended transfer where possible and report suspected disclosure through POL-09 immediately. They must preserve evidence and must not conceal or silently delete the incident history.
Evidence: Incident record linked to affected data and containment actions.

## 4.8 Privacy improvements
REQ-POL-06-08 | Recommended
Employees may suggest removal of unnecessary personal-data fields from forms, reports or dashboards to the relevant process owner.
Evidence: Optional data-minimization suggestion.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
