# Workplace Conduct and Conflicts of Interest

Document POL-02 | Version 2.0 | Approved | Effective 2026-09-01
Owner: HR Executive | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: All employees
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: No scheduled expiry

## Purpose and application
Protect fair treatment and impartial decisions in interactions with colleagues, customers, suppliers and merchants.

## 4.1 Gift declaration
REQ-POL-02-01 | Mandatory
Employees must declare any offered gift valued above PKR 1,000 to HR before accepting it. Cash and cash-equivalent gifts are prohibited at any value; splitting a gift does not avoid this rule.
Evidence: Gift register entry or documented refusal.

## 4.2 Respectful conduct
REQ-POL-02-02 | Mandatory
Employees must communicate respectfully and must not harass, threaten or discriminate against another person. A witness must report concerns through the confidential HR channel without circulating the allegation to colleagues.
Evidence: Restricted concern record with date and reporting channel.

## 4.3 Personal interests
REQ-POL-02-03 | Mandatory
Employees must disclose a personal or financial relationship with a merchant or supplier before participating in its selection, payment or complaint decision. The manager must assign an independent decision-maker.
Evidence: Disclosure and reassignment record.

## 4.4 Accurate records
REQ-POL-02-04 | Mandatory
Employees must record actual events and must not fabricate delivery attempts, training completion, assessment results or approvals. Corrections must retain the original entry and the reason for amendment.
Evidence: Original and corrected entries with actor and timestamp.

## 4.5 Confidential reporting
REQ-POL-02-05 | Mandatory
Employees must use the confidential HR channel to report misconduct. If the concern involves HR, the report must go directly to the Managing Director.
Evidence: Access-restricted report and assigned independent reviewer.

## 4.6 Non retaliation
REQ-POL-02-06 | Mandatory
Managers must not disadvantage a person for raising a good-faith concern or participating in an investigation. Any alleged retaliation must be referred to the Managing Director.
Evidence: Independent review record of any retaliation allegation.

## 4.7 Public statements
REQ-POL-02-07 | Mandatory
Employees must obtain Managing Director approval before representing the company publicly or publishing customer-related material. Personal accounts must not disclose internal records.
Evidence: Approved communication and approval reference.

## 4.8 Constructive feedback
REQ-POL-02-08 | Recommended
Employees are encouraged to discuss routine process frustrations privately with their manager and suggest a specific improvement supported by an example.
Evidence: Optional improvement suggestion.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
