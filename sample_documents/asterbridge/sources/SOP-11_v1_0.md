# Customer Support Case Handling Procedure

Document SOP-11 | Version 1.0 | Approved | Effective 2026-09-01
Owner: Operations Manager | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: Customer Support Executive
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: No scheduled expiry

## Purpose and application
Resolve customer enquiries with verified identity, accurate case records and escalation of issues outside delegated authority.

Role mandate: Resolve shipment enquiries and complaints within delegated authority. Reports to: Operations Manager. Department: Customer Experience. Delegated authority is limited to the steps below; independent financial or policy approval remains with the named approver.

## 4.1 Open and classify
REQ-SOP-11-01 | Mandatory
The Customer Support Executive must open a case for each new enquiry, record the shipment ID and classify the issue before advising the customer. Related follow-ups must link to the existing case.
Evidence: Case ID, category and linked follow-ups.

## 4.2 Verify the requester
REQ-SOP-11-02 | Mandatory
The Customer Support Executive must complete approved requester verification before disclosing protected shipment details, following POL-06. A failed verification permits only general public guidance.
Evidence: Verification outcome and disclosure scope.

## 4.3 Use current evidence
REQ-SOP-11-03 | Mandatory
The Customer Support Executive must check the latest recorded shipment event and applicable SOP before promising a next step. Missing tracking information must be described as unconfirmed.
Evidence: Source event and advice recorded in the case.

## 4.4 Acknowledge complaints
REQ-SOP-11-04 | Mandatory
The Customer Support Executive must acknowledge a complaint within 2 working hours of receipt and provide the case reference and next update time. Critical incidents follow POL-09 instead of this routine target.
Evidence: Receipt and acknowledgement timestamps.

## 4.5 Respect refund authority
REQ-SOP-11-05 | Mandatory
The Customer Support Executive must send refund requests to the Returns Specialist and must not promise eligibility, amount or payment date before approval under SOP-14 and SOP-16.
Evidence: Referral and approved customer response.

## 4.6 Escalate unresolved work
REQ-SOP-11-06 | Mandatory
The Customer Support Executive must escalate a routine case still unresolved after 1 working day to the Operations Manager with actions taken and the outstanding decision.
Evidence: Escalation record and receiving owner.

## 4.7 Close with evidence
REQ-SOP-11-07 | Mandatory
The Customer Support Executive must record the resolution, supporting evidence and customer notification before closure. Lack of customer response alone must not be recorded as successful delivery.
Evidence: Closure evidence and notification record.

## 4.8 Improve explanations
REQ-SOP-11-08 | Recommended
The Customer Support Executive may propose an FAQ clarification after identifying repeated confusion, subject to owner review before publication.
Evidence: Optional FAQ improvement request.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
