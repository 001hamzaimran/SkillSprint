# Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy

Document POL-05 | Version 2.0 | Approved | Effective 2026-09-01
Owner: IT Support Engineer | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: All employees
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: No scheduled expiry

## Purpose and application
Protect company systems through individual access, approved devices and prompt reporting of suspected compromise.

## 4.1 Screen lock
REQ-POL-05-01 | Mandatory
Employees must lock an unattended workstation immediately and configure automatic locking after no more than 5 minutes of inactivity. Shared warehouse terminals follow the same limit.
Evidence: Device setting check and observed lock demonstration.

## 4.2 Individual accounts
REQ-POL-05-02 | Mandatory
Employees must use individually assigned accounts and must not share passwords or authentication codes. Service accounts require a named IT owner and must not be used for routine interactive work.
Evidence: Account owner record and access review.

## 4.3 Multifactor authentication
REQ-POL-05-03 | Mandatory
Employees must activate multifactor authentication before accessing email, remote access or systems holding customer records. IT must provide an approved recovery method rather than disabling the requirement.
Evidence: MFA enrollment and recovery-method confirmation.

## 4.4 Approved devices
REQ-POL-05-04 | Mandatory
Employees must use company-managed devices or an explicitly approved device profile to access restricted data. Personal storage services must not receive company files.
Evidence: Asset or device approval record.

## 4.5 Suspicious content
REQ-POL-05-05 | Mandatory
Employees must report suspicious links, attachments or unexpected authentication requests to IT without opening them. If already opened, the employee must report the action and follow the incident process in POL-09.
Evidence: Security report and incident reference where applicable.

## 4.6 Software installation
REQ-POL-05-06 | Mandatory
Employees must obtain IT approval before installing software or browser extensions on company devices. IT must verify business purpose and permitted access before approval.
Evidence: Software request and approval record.

## 4.7 Secrets handling
REQ-POL-05-07 | Mandatory
Employees must store API keys and service credentials only in approved secret storage and must not include them in source repositories, screenshots, training examples or support tickets.
Evidence: Secret storage reference and sanitized support evidence.

## 4.8 Security practice
REQ-POL-05-08 | Recommended
Employees are encouraged to complete optional phishing simulations and discuss the warning signs with IT after the exercise.
Evidence: Optional simulation participation.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
