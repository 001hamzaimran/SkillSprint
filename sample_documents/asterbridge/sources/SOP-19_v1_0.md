# Analytics Data Access and Reporting Procedure

Document SOP-19 | Version 1.0 | Approved | Effective 2026-09-01
Owner: Operations Manager | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: Data Analyst
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: No scheduled expiry

## Purpose and application
Produce reproducible operational reports from authorized data with clear definitions, quality checks and privacy protections.

Role mandate: Produce reliable operational analysis using approved and minimized data. Reports to: Operations Manager. Department: Business Intelligence. Delegated authority is limited to the steps below; independent financial or policy approval remains with the named approver.

## 4.1 Approve analysis purpose
REQ-SOP-19-01 | Mandatory
The Data Analyst must document the business question, requested fields and intended recipients before receiving restricted data. The Operations Manager must approve the scope.
Evidence: Approved analysis request.

## 4.2 Use approved sources
REQ-SOP-19-02 | Mandatory
The Data Analyst must use approved source datasets and record their extraction time and version. An unverified spreadsheet must not silently replace an authoritative dataset.
Evidence: Dataset lineage and source version.

## 4.3 Minimize identifiers
REQ-SOP-19-03 | Mandatory
The Data Analyst must remove direct identifiers unless the approved question requires them. Group-level reporting must suppress cells containing fewer than 5 individuals or shipments where re-identification is plausible.
Evidence: Reviewed output and suppression record.

## 4.4 Define measures
REQ-SOP-19-04 | Mandatory
The Data Analyst must document numerator, denominator, time window and exclusions for each published performance measure. A zero denominator must be shown as not applicable rather than a fabricated percentage.
Evidence: Metric definition and boundary-case check.

## 4.5 Validate data quality
REQ-SOP-19-05 | Mandatory
The Data Analyst must check missing identifiers, duplicate events and inconsistent timestamps before publication. Material limitations must appear with the report.
Evidence: Quality-check results and disclosed limitations.

## 4.6 Review publication
REQ-SOP-19-06 | Mandatory
The Data Analyst must obtain owner review before publishing a report used for merchant or employee decisions. Review must cover interpretation, privacy and data quality.
Evidence: Report approval and version.

## 4.7 Dispose of extracts
REQ-SOP-19-07 | Mandatory
The Data Analyst must register temporary customer-data extracts and delete them within POL-06 limits unless an identified preservation hold applies.
Evidence: Export lifecycle and deletion or hold evidence.

## 4.8 Explore improvements
REQ-SOP-19-08 | Recommended
The Data Analyst may explore additional aggregate measures in a clearly labelled experimental report that is not used for operational decisions before review.
Evidence: Optional experimental analysis.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
