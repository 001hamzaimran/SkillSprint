# IT Access Provisioning and Support Procedure

Document SOP-18 | Version 1.0 | Approved | Effective 2026-09-01
Owner: Operations Manager | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: IT Support Engineer
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: No scheduled expiry

## Purpose and application
Provision approved access, record support work and remove access at the correct time without weakening security controls.

Role mandate: Provision least-privilege access and restore approved services safely. Reports to: Operations Manager. Department: Technology. Delegated authority is limited to the steps below; independent financial or policy approval remains with the named approver.

## 4.1 Validate access request
REQ-SOP-18-01 | Mandatory
The IT Support Engineer must verify employee identity, current role and manager approval before provisioning access. A support message without an approved request is insufficient.
Evidence: Approved access ticket and identity verification outcome.

## 4.2 Apply least privilege
REQ-SOP-18-02 | Mandatory
The IT Support Engineer must assign only the approved role permissions and record any time-limited exception with expiry and approving owner. Administrative access must not be the default.
Evidence: Permission assignment and exception expiry where applicable.

## 4.3 Enable security controls
REQ-SOP-18-03 | Mandatory
The IT Support Engineer must confirm MFA, screen lock and approved-device controls before handing over an account or device, following POL-05.
Evidence: Provisioning security checklist.

## 4.4 Verify recovery identity
REQ-SOP-18-04 | Mandatory
The IT Support Engineer must verify the account holder through the approved recovery workflow before resetting credentials. They must never ask the user to disclose an existing password or authentication code.
Evidence: Recovery verification outcome and reset audit entry.

## 4.5 Record support changes
REQ-SOP-18-05 | Mandatory
The IT Support Engineer must record affected service, change, approver and rollback action for support work that changes configuration. Emergency changes require incident-lead authorization and subsequent documentation.
Evidence: Change ticket and rollback record.

## 4.6 Revoke departing access
REQ-SOP-18-06 | Mandatory
The IT Support Engineer must revoke employee access at the approved exit effective time and confirm completion to HR. An urgent termination notice requires immediate revocation and incident escalation if access remains active.
Evidence: Revocation timestamps and HR confirmation.

## 4.7 Review permissions
REQ-SOP-18-07 | Mandatory
The IT Support Engineer must perform a monthly access review with managers and remove unapproved or expired permissions after recording the decision.
Evidence: Access review and removal evidence.

## 4.8 Improve support guidance
REQ-SOP-18-08 | Recommended
The IT Support Engineer may publish an approved self-service guide for recurring issues, excluding secrets and privileged operational details.
Evidence: Optional reviewed support guide.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
