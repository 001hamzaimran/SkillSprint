# Records Retention and Document Control Policy

Document POL-07 | Version 1.0 | Superseded | Effective 2026-07-01
Owner: Operations Manager | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: All employees
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: 2026-09-01 (exclusive)

## Purpose and application
Maintain usable operational evidence, apply the internal retention schedule and prevent uncontrolled deletion or silent alteration.

## 4.1 Routine operational retention
REQ-POL-07-01 | Mandatory
Record owners must retain routine delivery-event records for 90 calendar days after case closure. A documented preservation hold overrides routine deletion until the hold is formally released.
Evidence: Record category, closure date, retention deadline and any hold.

## 4.2 Training retention
REQ-POL-07-02 | Mandatory
HR must retain onboarding acknowledgements, assessment attempts and readiness decisions for 24 months after the employee leaves the company, unless a preservation hold applies.
Evidence: Training archive and calculated retention deadline.

## 4.3 Controlled repository
REQ-POL-07-03 | Mandatory
Employees must store operational evidence in the approved case or document repository. A local copy must not become the sole record of a decision or transaction.
Evidence: Repository link attached to the case.

## 4.4 Record correction
REQ-POL-07-04 | Mandatory
Record owners must correct errors through an auditable amendment recording author, time and reason. Original transaction values must not be overwritten without history.
Evidence: Amendment history preserving the original record.

## 4.5 Preservation holds
REQ-POL-07-05 | Mandatory
The Operations Manager must identify scope, owner and start date when issuing a preservation hold. Record owners must suspend scheduled deletion for the named records until written release.
Evidence: Hold register and release decision.

## 4.6 Secure disposal
REQ-POL-07-06 | Mandatory
Record owners must use approved disposal methods and retain a disposal log identifying category, date range, authorization and completion date. Disposal must not expose readable personal data.
Evidence: Authorized disposal log.

## 4.7 Retention uncertainty
REQ-POL-07-07 | Mandatory
Employees must refer records with no applicable retention category to the Operations Manager before deletion. They must not infer a deadline from an unrelated record type.
Evidence: Unclassified-record review and assigned retention category.

## 4.8 Archive review
REQ-POL-07-08 | Recommended
Record owners are encouraged to review archive searchability quarterly and propose improvements to naming or indexing.
Evidence: Optional archive review notes.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
