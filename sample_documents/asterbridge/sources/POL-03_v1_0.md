# Employee Onboarding and Competency Policy

Document POL-03 | Version 1.0 | Superseded | Effective 2026-07-01
Owner: HR Executive | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: All employees
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: 2026-09-01 (exclusive)

## Purpose and application
Ensure onboarding produces demonstrated competence with a traceable record of learning, assessment and manager review.

## 4.1 Core orientation
REQ-POL-03-01 | Mandatory
Employees must complete core orientation within 3 working days of joining. HR must schedule a replacement session within that deadline if the original session is cancelled.
Evidence: Attendance and completion record for core orientation.

## 4.2 Individual plan
REQ-POL-03-02 | Mandatory
HR must assign an onboarding plan based on role, experience, location and applicable requirements. Prior experience may change practice difficulty but must not remove mandatory acknowledgements.
Evidence: Employee-specific plan and applicability rationale.

## 4.3 Learning stages
REQ-POL-03-03 | Mandatory
Training managers must distribute learning across Day 1, Week 1, Week 2 and First 30 Days. Mandatory operational deadlines and prerequisites take priority over a convenient stage label.
Evidence: Staged plan with prerequisite links and due dates.

## 4.4 Knowledge assessment
REQ-POL-03-04 | Mandatory
Employees must score at least 80 percent on mandatory knowledge assessments. A failed attempt requires targeted revision and another attempt; the original result must remain recorded.
Evidence: Attempt history and linked revision assignment.

## 4.5 Practical competence
REQ-POL-03-05 | Mandatory
Employees must demonstrate the critical steps of a role task to an authorized assessor before independent work. A numerical score cannot compensate for a failed safety or privacy critical step.
Evidence: Rubric showing observed steps and assessor decision.

## 4.6 Completion integrity
REQ-POL-03-06 | Mandatory
Reviewers must verify required evidence before recording completion. Reading a module alone does not demonstrate practical competence or substitute for a required policy acknowledgement.
Evidence: Completion record referencing the required evidence type.

## 4.7 Readiness review
REQ-POL-03-07 | Mandatory
Managers must review employee readiness by the end of the first 30 calendar days and document remaining gaps, supervised-work limits and follow-up dates.
Evidence: Readiness review and any outstanding action plan.

## 4.8 Personal development
REQ-POL-03-08 | Recommended
Employees may select an optional development topic after agreeing the time allocation with their manager; optional learning must not delay mandatory training.
Evidence: Optional development agreement.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
