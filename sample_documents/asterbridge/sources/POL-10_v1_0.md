# Business Continuity and Service Recovery Policy

Document POL-10 | Version 1.0 | Superseded | Effective 2026-07-01
Owner: Operations Manager | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: All employees
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: 2026-09-01 (exclusive)

## Purpose and application
Maintain essential delivery operations during system outages and reconcile temporary work safely when service returns.

## 4.1 Outage checkpoints
REQ-POL-10-01 | Mandatory
During an activated continuity event, the incident lead must issue an internal status checkpoint at least every 60 elapsed minutes until normal service resumes. A checkpoint must state current impact, owner and next update time.
Evidence: Time-stamped continuity checkpoints.

## 4.2 Continuity activation
REQ-POL-10-02 | Mandatory
Only the duty Operations Manager may activate the continuity procedure after assessing service impact. If immediate danger exists, employees must take the safety actions in POL-08 without waiting for activation.
Evidence: Activation decision and incident reference.

## 4.3 Approved fallback
REQ-POL-10-03 | Mandatory
Employees must use only the approved numbered fallback forms or controlled offline register during an outage. Personal notebooks and unapproved messaging groups must not hold customer records.
Evidence: Fallback register with assigned custodian.

## 4.4 Minimum fallback data
REQ-POL-10-04 | Mandatory
Employees must record only shipment ID, action, time and assigned operator in the fallback register. Additional personal data requires explicit incident-lead approval and privacy controls.
Evidence: Fallback record and any additional-data approval.

## 4.5 Duplicate prevention
REQ-POL-10-05 | Mandatory
Employees must check shipment and transaction identifiers before entering recovered work into the restored system. Potential duplicates must be held for review rather than posted again.
Evidence: Reconciliation result and duplicate hold queue.

## 4.6 Recovery verification
REQ-POL-10-06 | Mandatory
The incident lead must obtain an IT health check and an operational sample check before declaring normal service restored. A login screen alone is not evidence of end-to-end recovery.
Evidence: Technical and operational recovery evidence.

## 4.7 Fallback reconciliation
REQ-POL-10-07 | Mandatory
Assigned record owners must reconcile fallback entries within 1 working day of restoration, resolve mismatches and dispose of temporary copies according to POL-06 and POL-07.
Evidence: Signed reconciliation with mismatch decisions.

## 4.8 Continuity improvements
REQ-POL-10-08 | Recommended
Employees may propose a simpler fallback step after a rehearsal, provided the proposal preserves privacy, traceability and independent approval.
Evidence: Optional continuity improvement proposal.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
