# People Operations Employee Lifecycle Procedure

Document SOP-17 | Version 1.0 | Approved | Effective 2026-09-01
Owner: Operations Manager | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: HR Executive
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: No scheduled expiry

## Purpose and application
Keep employee profiles, onboarding assignments and exit actions accurate while limiting access to sensitive records.

Role mandate: Maintain employee lifecycle records and coordinate required training. Reports to: Operations Manager. Department: People Operations. Delegated authority is limited to the steps below; independent financial or policy approval remains with the named approver.

## 4.1 Create employee profile
REQ-SOP-17-01 | Mandatory
The HR Executive must record approved role, department, manager, location and joining date before creating an onboarding assignment. Only information necessary for training may be exposed to the learning application.
Evidence: Approved employee profile and minimized learning record.

## 4.2 Assign requirements
REQ-SOP-17-02 | Mandatory
The HR Executive must map the current approved role requirements to each joining employee and identify any location or temporary-role conditions. Unresolved applicability must go to the requirement owner.
Evidence: Role mapping and applicability review.

## 4.3 Arrange orientation
REQ-SOP-17-03 | Mandatory
The HR Executive must schedule core orientation within POL-03 deadlines and monitor attendance. A missed session requires a replacement assignment and visible overdue status where relevant.
Evidence: Orientation schedule and attendance follow-up.

## 4.4 Track acknowledgements
REQ-SOP-17-04 | Mandatory
The HR Executive must retain acknowledgements against the exact policy version shown to the employee. A policy replacement requires evaluation of whether renewed acknowledgement is necessary.
Evidence: Version-specific acknowledgement record.

## 4.5 Coordinate role changes
REQ-SOP-17-05 | Mandatory
The HR Executive must notify the manager and IT of an approved role change before its effective time so training and access can be reviewed together.
Evidence: Role-change notice and receiving acknowledgements.

## 4.6 Initiate exit actions
REQ-SOP-17-06 | Mandatory
The HR Executive must notify IT of the approved exit effective time before that time and track asset return and access revocation. Urgent exits require immediate coordinated notification.
Evidence: Exit checklist and IT revocation confirmation.

## 4.7 Protect employee records
REQ-SOP-17-07 | Mandatory
The HR Executive must restrict sensitive employee records and provide managers only the information needed for an assigned decision. Access requests outside that scope require review.
Evidence: Access decision and disclosure record.

## 4.8 Collect onboarding feedback
REQ-SOP-17-08 | Recommended
The HR Executive may invite feedback after the first month and summarize improvement themes without exposing individual sensitive details.
Evidence: Optional feedback summary.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
