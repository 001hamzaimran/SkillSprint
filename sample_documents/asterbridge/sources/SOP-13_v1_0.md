# Warehouse Receipt Storage and Release Procedure

Document SOP-13 | Version 1.0 | Approved | Effective 2026-09-01
Owner: Operations Manager | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: Warehouse Associate
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: No scheduled expiry

## Purpose and application
Maintain parcel identity, condition and custody from receipt through storage and release.

Role mandate: Receive, store and dispatch parcels with a complete custody record. Reports to: Operations Manager. Department: Fulfilment. Delegated authority is limited to the steps below; independent financial or policy approval remains with the named approver.

## 4.1 Inspect inbound parcels
REQ-SOP-13-01 | Mandatory
The Warehouse Associate must check shipment label, count and visible condition at receipt. Damaged or unidentified parcels must be isolated pending supervisor instructions.
Evidence: Receipt scan and exception record.

## 4.2 Scan storage movement
REQ-SOP-13-02 | Mandatory
The Warehouse Associate must scan both shipment identifier and destination bin for every storage movement. A failed scan requires a controlled exception record before physical movement.
Evidence: Movement event or approved scan exception.

## 4.3 Segregate exceptions
REQ-SOP-13-03 | Mandatory
The Warehouse Associate must place damaged, disputed and unverified parcels in the designated exception area. These parcels must not return to normal stock without recorded release authority.
Evidence: Exception location and release decision.

## 4.4 Use safe handling
REQ-SOP-13-04 | Mandatory
The Warehouse Associate must follow posted zone controls and the task assessment in POL-08 before moving parcels. Only authorized staff may operate powered handling equipment.
Evidence: Safety briefing and handling authorization where required.

## 4.5 Match release instruction
REQ-SOP-13-05 | Mandatory
The Warehouse Associate must match the shipment to the approved manifest before release and must not release a parcel based solely on a verbal request.
Evidence: Manifest reference and release scan.

## 4.6 Record discrepancies
REQ-SOP-13-06 | Mandatory
The Warehouse Associate must notify the Dispatch Coordinator and supervisor of count or identity discrepancies before handover. The affected parcels remain held until reconciliation.
Evidence: Discrepancy report and hold resolution.

## 4.7 Perform assigned counts
REQ-SOP-13-07 | Mandatory
The Warehouse Associate must complete assigned cycle counts using physical observation and record differences without altering the expected quantity to match the count.
Evidence: Count sheet and variance investigation.

## 4.8 Suggest storage improvements
REQ-SOP-13-08 | Recommended
The Warehouse Associate may propose bin-layout improvements after observing repeated mis-picks; relocation requires supervisor approval and updated bin records.
Evidence: Optional layout proposal.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
