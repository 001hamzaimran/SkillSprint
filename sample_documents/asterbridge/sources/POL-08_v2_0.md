# Workplace Safety and Visitor Policy

Document POL-08 | Version 2.0 | Approved | Effective 2026-09-01
Owner: Operations Manager | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: All employees and supervised visitors
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: No scheduled expiry

## Purpose and application
Provide clear stop-work, visitor-control and emergency actions for office and warehouse activities.

## 4.1 Safety concern reporting
REQ-POL-08-01 | Mandatory
Employees must report a non-urgent safety concern before the end of the current shift. An immediate danger requires stopping the activity and alerting the shift supervisor at once.
Evidence: Hazard report with discovery and notification times.

## 4.2 Emergency evacuation
REQ-POL-08-02 | Mandatory
Employees must stop work and use the signed evacuation route when the alarm sounds. They must report to the assembly point and must not re-enter until the incident lead authorizes it.
Evidence: Evacuation drill or incident attendance record.

## 4.3 Restricted equipment
REQ-POL-08-03 | Mandatory
Employees must not operate powered warehouse equipment without documented authorization and task-specific training. Observing another operator does not grant authorization.
Evidence: Equipment authorization and competency record.

## 4.4 Personal protective equipment
REQ-POL-08-04 | Mandatory
Employees entering marked warehouse handling zones must wear the posted protective equipment. The supervisor must stop the task if suitable equipment is unavailable.
Evidence: Zone briefing and equipment check.

## 4.5 Visitor escort
REQ-POL-08-05 | Mandatory
Employees hosting a visitor must arrange registration, a visible visitor pass and an escort in restricted areas. Visitors must not use employee access credentials.
Evidence: Visitor entry, host and exit record.

## 4.6 Manual handling
REQ-POL-08-06 | Mandatory
Employees must assess parcel weight and route before lifting and must request approved handling assistance when the posted safe-handling limit is exceeded. They must not invent a universal weight limit where a task assessment is required.
Evidence: Handling assessment or observed safe-handling demonstration.

## 4.7 Near misses
REQ-POL-08-07 | Mandatory
Employees must record near misses even when no injury or damage occurs. The supervisor must assign a corrective action owner and verify completion.
Evidence: Near-miss record and closed corrective action.

## 4.8 Safety suggestions
REQ-POL-08-08 | Recommended
Employees are encouraged to suggest improvements to workstation layout or parcel movement after discussing them with the supervisor.
Evidence: Optional safety suggestion.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
