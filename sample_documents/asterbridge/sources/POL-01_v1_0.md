# Employee Handbook and Document Governance

Document POL-01 | Version 1.0 | Superseded | Effective 2026-07-01
Owner: HR Executive | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: All employees
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: 2026-09-01 (exclusive)

## Purpose and application
Establish how employees find authoritative instructions, acknowledge onboarding obligations and raise uncertainty before performing restricted work.

## 4.1 Handbook acknowledgement
REQ-POL-01-01 | Mandatory
Employees must acknowledge the current handbook within 2 working days of joining. If access is unavailable, HR records the outage and obtains a supervised paper acknowledgement within the same period.
Evidence: Dated acknowledgement linked to the employee and handbook version.

## 4.2 Authoritative instructions
REQ-POL-01-02 | Mandatory
Employees must follow the latest effective approved policy, then the applicable SOP, then an approved role guide, then an FAQ. A lower-authority document cannot override a policy; unresolved conflicts go to the policy owner before action.
Evidence: Conflict record identifying both sources and the owner decision.

## 4.3 Controlled copies
REQ-POL-01-03 | Mandatory
Document owners must record document ID, owner, version, approval role and effective date before release. An unapproved draft must not be used to assign mandatory training.
Evidence: Document register entry and release decision.

## 4.4 Version replacement
REQ-POL-01-04 | Mandatory
Document owners must link each revision to the superseded version and identify changed sections. Employees must use the version effective on the date of the activity; historical copies remain available for audit.
Evidence: Version history with effective intervals and changed-section list.

## 4.5 Role assignment
REQ-POL-01-05 | Mandatory
HR must record each employee role, department, joining date and reporting manager before assigning onboarding. A temporary role change requires a recorded start date, end date and additional requirements.
Evidence: Employee profile and approved assignment record.

## 4.6 Restricted work
REQ-POL-01-06 | Mandatory
Employees must complete the relevant prerequisite training before independently performing restricted tasks. A manager may arrange supervised practice but must not mark an unmet prerequisite complete.
Evidence: Training record and named supervisor for any practice session.

## 4.7 Unclear requirements
REQ-POL-01-07 | Mandatory
Employees must pause the affected activity and ask the document owner when two equally authoritative instructions conflict or a necessary limit is missing. The owner must record the resolution before work resumes.
Evidence: Question, source references and documented resolution.

## 4.8 Onboarding buddy
REQ-POL-01-08 | Recommended
Employees are encouraged to arrange a weekly discussion with an experienced colleague during the first month to clarify workflows and identify useful practice.
Evidence: Optional buddy meeting notes.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
