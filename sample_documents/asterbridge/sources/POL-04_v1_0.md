# Leave Attendance and Shift Coverage Policy

Document POL-04 | Version 1.0 | Superseded | Effective 2026-07-01
Owner: HR Executive | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: All employees
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: 2026-09-01 (exclusive)

## Purpose and application
Make absence requests and shift handovers predictable while retaining a clear emergency route and reliable attendance records.

## 4.1 Planned absence notice
REQ-POL-04-01 | Mandatory
Employees must submit a planned absence request at least 5 working days before the proposed start. The manager may approve shorter notice only after recording a coverage arrangement.
Evidence: Request timestamp, decision and any coverage exception.

## 4.2 Approval before absence
REQ-POL-04-02 | Mandatory
Employees must obtain recorded approval before taking planned leave. Submission alone is not approval, and employees must not approve their own request.
Evidence: Request and independent approver decision.

## 4.3 Unexpected absence
REQ-POL-04-03 | Mandatory
Employees must notify the manager as soon as reasonably possible when an emergency prevents attendance. Where the employee cannot communicate, HR must accept notification from a nominated contact and record the circumstances.
Evidence: Time-stamped notification with emergency circumstances.

## 4.4 Shift handover
REQ-POL-04-04 | Mandatory
Employees must transfer open tasks, critical deadlines and escalation contacts to the designated cover before planned absence. Customer personal data must remain in approved systems.
Evidence: Handover record and receiving colleague acknowledgement.

## 4.5 Attendance correction
REQ-POL-04-05 | Mandatory
Employees must request correction of an inaccurate attendance entry within 2 working days of discovering it. HR must retain the original value and the evidence supporting correction.
Evidence: Correction request, original entry and audit history.

## 4.6 Minimum coverage
REQ-POL-04-06 | Mandatory
Managers must confirm required operational coverage before approving concurrent absences. If coverage cannot be arranged, the manager must record the reason and discuss alternative dates.
Evidence: Coverage assessment and recorded decision.

## 4.7 Sensitive absence details
REQ-POL-04-07 | Mandatory
HR must restrict medical or other sensitive absence details to authorized People Operations staff. Operational managers receive only information needed to arrange work.
Evidence: Restricted record and minimum-necessary manager notification.

## 4.8 Calendar planning
REQ-POL-04-08 | Recommended
Employees are encouraged to discuss anticipated leave early so teams can plan coverage before a formal request is submitted.
Evidence: Optional planning note.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
