# Dispatch Planning and Delivery Exception Procedure

Document SOP-12 | Version 1.0 | Approved | Effective 2026-09-01
Owner: Operations Manager | Approval authority: Managing Director | Scope: Dispatch Coordinator
Review due: 2027-09-01 | Valid until: No scheduled expiry

## Purpose and application
Release complete dispatch manifests and manage delivery exceptions without losing custody or bypassing safety controls.

Role mandate: Release accurate manifests and coordinate delivery exceptions. Reports to: Operations Manager. Department: Delivery Operations. Delegated authority is limited to the steps below; independent financial or policy approval remains with the named approver.

## 4.1 Validate readiness
REQ-SOP-12-01 | Mandatory
The Dispatch Coordinator must confirm that each shipment has a unique identifier, destination zone and warehouse release before adding it to a manifest. Incomplete shipments remain on hold.
Evidence: Readiness checklist and hold reason.

## 4.2 Check route capacity
REQ-SOP-12-02 | Mandatory
The Dispatch Coordinator must compare planned load against the approved vehicle and route capacity before release. Over-capacity work must be reassigned or referred to the Operations Manager.
Evidence: Capacity check and any reassignment.

## 4.3 Record custody transfer
REQ-SOP-12-03 | Mandatory
The Dispatch Coordinator must obtain operator acceptance for the manifest and record parcel count and handover time before departure. A count mismatch must be resolved before release.
Evidence: Accepted manifest and count reconciliation.

## 4.4 Restrict address changes
REQ-SOP-12-04 | Mandatory
The Dispatch Coordinator must route destination changes through Customer Support verification before amending a released manifest. Driver messages alone do not authorize a change.
Evidence: Verified change request and manifest history.

## 4.5 Record failed attempts
REQ-SOP-12-05 | Mandatory
The Dispatch Coordinator must record the actual reason and available evidence for a failed delivery attempt. A failed attempt must not be entered as delivered to close a route.
Evidence: Delivery exception and supporting event evidence.

## 4.6 Escalate lost custody
REQ-SOP-12-06 | Mandatory
The Dispatch Coordinator must stop further movement of affected records and notify the Operations Manager immediately when parcel custody cannot be established. Security or safety indicators also invoke POL-09.
Evidence: Custody incident and escalation.

## 4.7 Reconcile the route
REQ-SOP-12-07 | Mandatory
The Dispatch Coordinator must reconcile delivered, returned and outstanding parcels at shift end and assign an owner to every unresolved difference.
Evidence: Shift-end manifest reconciliation.

## 4.8 Review route patterns
REQ-SOP-12-08 | Recommended
The Dispatch Coordinator may summarize recurring route delays for the weekly operations review without including unnecessary customer identifiers.
Evidence: Optional route improvement summary.

## Training and control
Employees learn the applicable clauses during Week 1 and complete a source-based knowledge check. Task performers demonstrate the relevant procedure before independent work. Mandatory knowledge checks require at least 80 percent; all safety and privacy critical steps must pass. The assessor records requirement IDs, evidence and the source version. An earlier operational deadline still applies. Questions and exceptions follow POL-01; related controls include POL-01, POL-03, POL-05, POL-06 and POL-09.
